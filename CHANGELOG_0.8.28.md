# Ascendance Unity 0.8.28 — Exploration Deck Preview, Reorder & Search Integration

- Added a persistent real Exploration deck backed by unrevealed graph Location IDs.
- Explore now draws the next legal connected Location card; illegal cards are set aside and returned/reshuffled after a legal draw.
- SD005 Weathered Map reveals a connected Location but defers its Reveal Effect until entry/normal reveal.
- SD006 Forgotten Trail Marker inspects a connected hidden Location and lets the player reveal it or leave it hidden.
- SD010 Traveler's Journal exposes the top 2 Exploration cards and lets the player reorder them.
- Rowan Improved Trailblazer now uses the same live Exploration deck: once during the turn after Explore/Reveal, choose one of the top 2 to remain on top and bottom the other.
- Exploration deck order, deferred Reveal Effects, and unresolved choices persist through save/resume.
